## Cross-check

These 40 are the same questions as numbers 81 to 120 of
`2005-full-paper-120q-OFFICIAL-KEY.pdf`. Compared letter by letter against the
bold answers extracted from that file: **40 agreements out of 40, no
disagreements.**

So this file adds no new coverage. It is a second, independent printed key for
the finance block, and it confirms the first one exactly.

## Two things it does settle

1. **Asset-based financing is debt financing.** Question 19, printed in bold, on
   an official key. That is now confirmed by the notes, by deck 10 slide 35, and
   by two separate printed keys. The compiled answer sheet saying "equity" is
   wrong.
2. **Question 38 reproduces the known Kaneapa error.** The correct figure is
   December credit sales, $54,450. It is printed as option d in question 34 but
   not offered in question 38, and the key marks $78,650. The error is the
   paper's, not a transcription slip.
